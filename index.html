Reserve

Hold a table, then we’ll confirm.

Parties of 1 to 8. Ask at least 18 hours ahead — same rule as the book on sazon.gg. The table isn’t held until Sazón replies. Prefer to talk to someone? Call 01481 715287.

Larger parties, same-day tables and changes: call the restaurant. If you’re running late, they need to know.
